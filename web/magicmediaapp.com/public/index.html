Watch It Come Alive

Point your camera at any photo, poster, or object. Magic Media recognises it in real time and lays video, audio, or a 3D model right where you're looking.

No marker cards. No headset. Just the thing in front of you, opening up.

01 Premise

Every surface is already a screen.

On device

Camera frames are processed entirely on your iPhone or iPad for tracking. Nothing is recorded. Nothing is uploaded.

Instant

Recognition happens in real time against the live camera feed — the overlay is anchored the moment the surface is found.

Open

Attach your own video or audio to your own image and submit it. Once reviewed, anyone can discover it by scanning.
