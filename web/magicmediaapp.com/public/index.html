Who It's For

Where Printed Matter Still Does the Work

Magic Media pays off wherever something printed is already in someone's hands and there's more to say than the page has room for. These are the places we've seen that happen.

Strongest fit

Exhibits & Visitor Attractions

A wall label has room for a paragraph. Scanning it can play the excavation footage, the artist at work, or the animal in motion.

Museums · Science centers · Zoos & aquariums · Galleries · Historic sites · Botanical gardens

Manuals & Assembly Guides

Point the camera at step four and watch step four. Fewer support calls, fewer returns, fewer diagrams nobody can decipher.

Flat-pack furniture · Appliances · Bikes · Tools · Toys & kits · Instruments · Devices

Educational Print

Workbooks, lab manuals, and field guides where the still image is doing a job that really needs motion.

Curriculum publishers · STEM & robotics kits · Homeschool & Montessori · Lab manuals

Alumni & trade magazines · Visitor guides · Playbills · Event & sports programs

One honest filter, whatever your industry: this works best when the person holding your print is stopped, curious, and actually wants what's on the other side of the scan — a visitor at an exhibit, someone halfway through assembling a thing, a reader with the magazine open. It works least well as an interruption. If you can tell us about that moment, we can tell you quickly whether it's a fit.
